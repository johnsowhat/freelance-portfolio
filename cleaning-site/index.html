What's included

Every clean covers kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas. Deeper cleans go further.

Standard

  • Dusting of reachable surfaces
  • Vacuum and mop all floors
  • Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop, outside of appliances
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors
  • Beds made, trash taken out

Deep

  • Everything in Standard
  • Baseboards, door frames and light switches
  • Scale and grime removal in showers
  • Behind and under furniture you can move
  • Window sills and blinds

Move-in / move-out

  • Everything in Deep
  • Inside all cabinets and drawers
  • Inside fridge and oven
  • Walls spot-cleaned
  • Ready for your deposit inspection

Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients give us a door code or leave a key. You'll get a message when we arrive and when we're done.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, all supplies and equipment. Prefer your own products? Just leave them out with a note.

How do recurring discounts work?

Weekly cleans save 20%, every two weeks 15%, monthly 10%. You can pause or cancel anytime, no contract.

What if I need to reschedule?

Rescheduling is free up to 24 hours before your clean. Later changes have a $40 fee because the team has already planned the day.

Do you clean homes with pets?

Of course. Just let us know about them when you book so the team can plan for pet hair.
