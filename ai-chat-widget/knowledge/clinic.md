# Evergreen Family Dental (Portland, OR)

## Contact & location
Address: 1420 NW Lovejoy St, Suite 200, Portland, OR 97209. Free parking garage under the building, validate your ticket at the front desk.
Phone: (503) 555-0142. Email: hello@evergreendental.example
Hours: Mon–Thu 8:00–18:00, Fri 8:00–14:00. Closed weekends and federal holidays.
Emergency: existing patients can call the main number after hours and follow the emergency prompt.

## Services
- Check-up & cleaning (exam, X-rays, cleaning): $185 without insurance. Takes about 60 minutes.
- Teeth whitening (in-office, 1 visit): $450. Take-home kit: $250.
- Fillings (tooth-colored composite): from $160 per tooth.
- Crowns (same-day CEREC crowns): from $1,200.
- Invisalign clear aligners: from $4,500. Free consultation with a 3D scan.
- Dental implants: from $3,800 per implant including crown. Free consultation.
- Kids dentistry: from age 3. First visit for kids under 6 is free.
- Emergency visits (pain, broken tooth): same-day when possible, $95 exam fee.
We do NOT do: orthodontic braces with brackets, oral surgery for wisdom teeth (we refer to a partner surgeon), or sedation for adults.

## Insurance & payment
In-network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian and United Concordia. Out-of-network: we file the claim for you.
No insurance? Evergreen Membership Plan: $29/month, includes 2 cleanings per year, X-rays and 15% off all treatments.
Payment: cards, Apple Pay, HSA/FSA cards, and 0% financing for 12 months through CareCredit on treatments over $500.

## New patients

Cancellation: free up to 24 hours before. Late cancellation or no-show fee: $50.
